I do not have a perfect direction in mind for the game yet, but I have some ideas and some of the work for the not done ideas might go into future games. As for Xidoria: a 3d endless shooter arena style game with RPG elements and equipment. Not sure how that tags out exactly, but I want to have fun with it and so far I am!

10/9/2026 devlog

I have been working on this game for under a week now, and its been coming along pretty well! I have been intimidated as the line numbers get bigger and even the forethought is foretought. I want to avoid weird code and odd bugs for future development, which means I am trying to avoid janky work arounds in favor for things I understand more thouroughly. Aiming for simplicity and a little bit for elbow grease to ensure I learn a thing or two. So far the largest trouble has been models and the lack of them. I love drawing and spriting, but trying to do 3d modeling in blender has been troublesome. It has been fun aswell, but the challenge is there. I found it easiest to throw an idea out and try my best, I am trying to watch some videos to better my skills, but I fear I want to use some models that look good. For the time being I am using some billboarding images pulled off google, not the proudest, but it helps me code the bare bones. Hopefully I will remember to update this. If I don't, maybe email me?

Simple Defenders

Simple defenders is a simplistically styled tower defence game. With the help of my team we were able to build this game together. Although the game is unfinished, I am the only team member still working on a fork of the game on github. The biggest challenge was getting the whole team on the same page to work on the project. The best way we found to address the issue is strong communication, and someone to lead the development. I ended up leading the development and many of the towers are based off the Cannon tower I made, and most pushes are from combining everyone's branches.
